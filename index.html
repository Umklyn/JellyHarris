About Brussels, BE — Est. 2019

Cities
in black
& white.

[ Portrait ]

I document what cities don't pose for — the crowd that doesn't know it's being watched, the building catching the wrong light at the right moment, the pause between two sounds. Brussels first. Everywhere else after.

Photography as a way of slowing down. Finding the frame that makes the ordinary feel like it meant something all along.
