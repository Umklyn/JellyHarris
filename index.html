Jelly Harris

I photograph what people don't pose for.

A glance that slips past the lens before it has time to arrange itself. A hand caught mid-thought. The quiet between two people who've stopped performing for each other.

I'm drawn to solitude in a crowd, to light that lands somewhere it wasn't supposed to, to the second right before or right after something happens, when nobody's decided yet how to feel about it.

A photograph doesn't have to explain someone.

It just has to catch what was already there, and let the rest stay theirs.
